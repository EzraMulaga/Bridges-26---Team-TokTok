### Overview

The Emergency Info Holder is a lightweight system designed to provide critical medical information in emergency situations where a patient may be unable to communicate. The system prioritizes speed, accessibility, and reliability, especially in environments where access to centralized health systems or full medical records is limited. This project was developed as part of a hackathon-style event at the BRIDGES Summer School in the South Pacific (2026), where it was awarded First Place.

### Problem Definition

In emergency situations, responders often face the following constraints:
- The patient may be unconscious or disoriented
- There is no time to search multiple systems
- Full medical history is not accessible
- Emergency responders may not know the patient personally

These challenges are amplified in regions where:
- Digital health infrastructure is fragmented
- National identification coverage is incomplete
- Geography and funding limit rapid information access

### Limitations of Traditional Solutions
Common approaches include:
- Contacting family members or emergency contacts
- Carrying medical information on paper
However, these methods:
- Are slow or unreliable in urgent situations
- Depend on availability of third parties
- Are easily lost, damaged, or outdated

### Proposed Solution
The Emergency Info Holder uses a QR-code-based approach to provide fast access to essential medical information.

#### Key Features
- A QR code stored in a small, waterproof pouch
- Scanning the code opens a secure online emergency information page
- Information is limited to critical emergency data only (e.g. allergies, conditions, emergency contacts)

This design minimizes complexity while maximizing availability.

### Design Rationale
The solution is intentionally:
- **Low-cost** – suitable for wide adoption
- **Device-agnostic** – works with any smartphone
- **Offline-friendly in practice** – QR access is faster than system searches
- Independent of national ID systems

This makes it particularly suitable for contexts where:
- Not all citizens have formal identification
- Infrastructure varies by region

Ongoing Work
The project is currently being extended beyond the hackathon prototype, including:
- Improved data security and access control
- Cleaner UI for emergency responders
- Better separation between emergency data and full medical records
- Exploration of offline or cached access options

### Disclaimer
This project is a prototype and is not intended to replace professional medical systems. It is designed to supplement emergency response by providing immediate, high-priority information.
